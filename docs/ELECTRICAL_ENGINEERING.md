# Electrical Engineering — Detailed Specification

## Purpose

Provide a field reference and documentation workspace for electrical professionals. E-Tools is not a certified measuring instrument, electrical design approval system or substitute for current standards, manufacturer documentation and qualified judgement.

## Calculation tools

- Ohm's law and DC power.
- AC single-phase and three-phase power with explicit assumptions.
- Power factor, apparent/active/reactive power and energy.
- Voltage drop estimates with conductor material, cross-section, length, current and circuit assumptions.
- Cable/conductor reference tables with source/version metadata.
- Resistor, LED and basic low-voltage electronics helpers.
- Unit conversion and tolerance calculations.

Every result shows formula, input values, units, assumptions, rounding and warnings. Do not silently choose installation method, ambient temperature, grouping, correction factors or protective-device characteristics.

## Installation records

- Projects, sites, distribution boards and circuits.
- Circuit identifiers, phases, nominal ratings and destination.
- Cable type, conductor count, cross-section, route and length.
- Protective-device make/model, rating and reference data.
- Terminal, conductor and cable labels.
- Material lists and installation notes.
- PDF plan attachments with annotations and linked circuit records.

## Measurement records

- Manual measurement entry with instrument identity, serial number and calibration/status notes.
- Timestamp, operator, circuit/asset reference, unit and environmental notes.
- Attach photos and evidence.
- Configurable report templates and PDF/CSV export.
- Preserve original entries and record edits in an audit trail.

E-Tools must not claim to perform insulation, loop impedance, RCD trip-time, continuity or earth-electrode tests using the tablet alone. Such tests require suitable approved test equipment. Any future instrument integration must be model-specific, validated and clearly identify the instrument as the measurement source.

## Safety and compliance

- Show a clear warning that calculations are indicative and must be checked against current applicable standards and installation conditions.
- Do not generate a pass/fail compliance certificate from incomplete inputs.
- Never encourage opening energized equipment or probing hazardous circuits with general-purpose tablet adapters.
- Separate design estimates, manually entered readings and instrument-imported readings.
- Keep source/version metadata for reference tables and formulas.
- Require qualified personnel and appropriate test equipment for safety-critical work.
